# Concept Brief

[Project home](README.md) · Status: proposed pitch built on confirmed direction.

## Pitch

You arrive at a converted telecommunications office where people brought together by circumstance have made a home. A few months after an incident disrupted time and space, scattered refuges can reach one another only by radio. Ordinary broadcasts share the network with signals from other times and places, some intimately connected to the residents' lives.

Investigating those signals draws you into difficult histories. Each story follows someone dealing with an emotional burden and leaves a trace in the household. Standalone cases connect to a larger mystery whose cause is recorded separately in the [author-only account](incident-and-network-author-truth.md).

## Player promise

Become familiar with a household and its network, learn to investigate the lives behind the voices, and decide how to act when your discoveries affect people you depend on.

The desired emotional movement is curiosity → familiarity → unease → discovery → a changed relationship. Cases can include tenderness, humor, disappointment, and relief. Constant escalation would leave too little room to experience belonging.

## Confirmed foundations

- Found family and belonging are the emotional focus.
- Investigating people and uncovering unsettling truths are the preferred player experience.
- The Common House is the leading concept.
- The house and radio connections/investigations should be combined into a radio outpost.

## Format and audience

A single-player, first-person narrative investigation game with authored cases and a recurring home. First person is an expressed interest; this particular format remains a proposal. The audience hypothesis is players who enjoy close observation, character-driven mysteries, environmental storytelling, and unsettling audio. No audience research or commercial demand validation has been performed.

The POC will be developed by the user alone in Unity on their desktop rig. Commercial platforms, game length, price, release model, and localization are deferred until after the POC.

## Confirmed world and player direction

The setting is an alternate future after societal collapse. Safe pockets vary from a house to a county; the intervening terrain cannot be navigated reliably. You can leave a refuge unharmed and still never return. That impossibility echoes the stories' concern with the past and former selves. Arrivals have become rare; the player is one of them.

Residents have uneven knowledge of strange transmissions. The player is a newcomer, conceived as a blank slate for self-insertion. The tone favors suspense, mystery, and creepiness over fright. Stories are authored cases; open source is a theme of the world, not an engine or licensing requirement.

Present-day play remains at the outpost. Other locations are used infrequently for investigable flashbacks and memories. The nature and reliability of those experiences remain to be designed.

## Design pillars

| Pillar | Design obligation | Evidence to seek in a prototype |
|---|---|---|
| Investigation changes relationships | A discovery changes what can be asked, shared, or done with a person. | Players explain a relationship consequence using a specific discovery. |
| Belonging is reciprocal | Residents offer care and agency beyond rewarding successful work. | Players remember an act of care unrelated to solving the case. |
| Radio connects people | Relaying and arranging contact affect named people with needs. | Players can explain why they chose a recipient or withheld contact. |
| A familiar home holds contradictions | Ordinary routines make deviations noticeable. | Players notice an inconsistency without a quest marker identifying its meaning. |
| Evidence supports inference | Factual questions have discoverable answers; motives may remain contested. | Players cite clues and distinguish certainty from speculation. |

## Creative boundaries

These are proposed constraints for the first playable: a small cast, a compact house, one authored case, and no combat. Use household work to reveal relationships or information; avoid recurring chores that only delay investigation. Keep the community capable of care and change after uncomfortable truths surface.

Do not make every kind resident secretly malicious. Do not require the player to violate every boundary to reach the only satisfying ending. A refusal to disclose something is not itself proof of wrongdoing.

## Distinctive opportunity

The player works where they live. A call can alter the meaning of breakfast, a room, a shared tool, or an old nickname. Radio operations and domestic relationships therefore develop together.

The strongest initial question is whether connecting people can become an investigative decision with emotional consequences. We do not yet need a large map, extensive mythology, or generated cases to test that question.
