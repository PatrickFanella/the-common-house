# City assets for flashbacks

D-40 in the [decision register](decisions-and-open-questions.md) records the
owner's expectation of a good number of flashbacks and request for city assets.
[Issue #90](https://git.subcult.tv/PatrickFanella/the-common-house/issues/90)
owns this first asset-kit slice.

The proposed study is a compact city street with mixed brick and concrete
fronts, small storefronts, office windows, a service alley, and ordinary street
furniture. It gives later scenes reusable forms while the writing develops.
The street is a review composition, not an established location. City, country,
calendar, former businesses, and which stories use these assets remain open.

Keep D-24's low-poly geometry, small textures, vertex lighting, and soft frame.
Use a quieter public-street palette than the home's handmade graphics: brick,
concrete, asphalt, dull metal, timber, and restrained painted fixtures. Generic
shop and bus signs establish functions without naming companies or routes.
Window panes are opaque surface studies; building shells have no interiors.

## First kit

| Group | Modules |
|---|---|
| Street surfaces | Straight road, marked crossing, pavement with curb, service alley with drain |
| Facade bays | Brick window, concrete window, office window, storefront, closed shutter, closed doorway |
| Building dressing | Roof cap with parapet/vent, fire-escape landing with ladder |
| Street furniture | Streetlamp, utility pole, bus shelter, bench, bollard, litter bin, utility cabinet, public notice board |

The modules are original procedural prefabs with shared materials. Their grid
and practical use are in the [asset guide](../development/city-assets.md).
The fire escape is dressing, not a qualified climbing route. Fixtures and
wires do not simulate power, traffic, or network behavior.

## Flashback boundary

This request revises the earlier expectation of infrequent flashbacks. It does
not settle their access mechanism, perspective, reliability, source, or case
content. The [world bible](narrative-and-world-bible.md) and
[case-writing guide](episodic-story-and-case-writing.md) still own those rules.
An asset study does not imply that a past event is visible without a source.

Present-day play remains at the home base. CityKitReview is separate from the
playable build, with no protagonist, NPCs, evidence, interaction targets, or
flashback transitions. Individual authored scenes can later select and adapt
these modules without adopting this street's layout as canon.
