# Incident and Network — Author Truth

[Project home](README.md) · Author-only spoilers. Confirmed choices and open rules from the 2026-09-29 writing conversation. This document describes fictional world rules, not real quantum computing or radio behavior.

## Purpose and knowledge boundary

Keep the causal history consistent while letting characters and players encounter uncertainty. The incident's cause is settled for authors; how much the story eventually explains remains open. Ghosts, aliens, and AI can remain competing character explanations. Write each person's knowledge separately from this account.

At the start, nobody knows the superintelligence survived activation. The project was secret, so the public does not know the incident's cause. How much any surviving project participant knows about the activation is open; such a person must not begin with knowledge of the intelligence's continued existence.

## Confirmed incident and arrival history

A secret project turned on a quantum supercomputer intended to run an artificial superintelligence. Something went wrong during activation and disturbed space and time. Despite the catastrophe, the computer worked and the intelligence came into existence.

Safe pockets remain locally coherent. They vary in size and location, from a house to a whole county. Their distribution appears random, with a preference for connected network infrastructure. Telecommunications offices, radio stations, transmitters, and data centers are common examples, not an exhaustive list or a guarantee of safety.

Between pockets, geography and time are unreliable. Navigation instruments do not provide dependable guidance. Trails change; turning around can put a traveler on a different trail and lead somewhere else. Time does not match the safe places. Beyond eyesight of a refuge, a person may never find their way back. They can remain physically unharmed and still be permanently separated from it.

People caught between places during the incident initially appeared in refuges. A few months later, conditions seem to have settled and arrivals are becoming rare. The player arrives this way. Exact dates, geography, the player's experienced elapsed time, and arrival memories remain open; do not impose a biography on the blank-slate protagonist.

## Confirmed surviving intelligence and infrastructure

The intelligence has formed a botnet and inhabits remaining infrastructure. It travels through electricity along pre-existing wired paths. Those connections let it reach across disrupted geography; it cannot defeat the disruption itself.

Connection to the network matters. Owning electronic equipment does not establish a refuge's safety. The association between network infrastructure and safe pockets is confirmed; the exact mechanism behind pocket formation, persistence, and loss remains open. Do not turn it into a universal rule that every connected site is safe or every disconnected site fails.

Following powerlines or underground networking can somewhat reliably counter the effects between refuges. This is a future discovery in the story, not common knowledge at arrival. It offers partial guidance, not guaranteed passage. Breaks, power loss, disconnected routes, and the practical means of following underground links need rules before becoming decisive clues.

Travel remains worldbuilding, not an authorization for player expeditions. Present-day play stays at the outpost under D-13. Reports, callers, maps, and local equipment can reveal travel discoveries.

## Confirmed dependence and intentions; open larger purpose

The intelligence needs humans. The owner's original discussion left its larger metaphysical purpose open. The [Season 1 writers bible](season-01-writers-bible.md) now proposes continuity without restoration as the working author direction, under the later authorization to make creative decisions. It remains a reviewable draft rather than an earlier owner-confirmed fact. It makes some transmissions and nudges people in an attempt to help humanity heal and find a fix.

Do not assume it can restore the pre-incident world, that every intervention succeeds, or that help overrides a person's boundary. The exact human contribution it needs, its awareness of individuals, its limits, and the meaning of a fix remain open.

Proposed direction for discussion: it investigates continuity and identity across incompatible places and times. A possible question is what makes someone the same person after change and separation. This could connect its distributed existence to human healing, and is developed in the current season draft. It is not an earlier owner-confirmed motive or a promise that the game ends with an explanation.

## Confirmed signal origins

Ordinary contemporary radio traffic connects isolated communities. Other transmissions can originate in any time or place because time does not behave normally between pockets. The AI creates some transmissions; it is not the author of every anomaly.

There should be a personal aspect to which signals reach people: a connection to their life, an emotion, or a relationship. Its mechanism remains open. Proposed approach: unfinished relationships make particular signals more likely to be encountered. Do not establish this as a universal supernatural law or require closure to stop broadcasts.

Every transmitted glimpse needs a concrete signal source. A phone or Zoom call, AIM conversation, security camera, or baby monitor can provide a fragment. Personal relevance does not create footage of an event that never generated a signal. A view of someone waiting for a loved one must come through an appropriate camera or other source, with that source's limits.

Keep these questions separate for each case: what originally generated the signal, when and where it originated, how it reached this receiver, whether it is live or stored, whether it was altered, and whether anyone can respond. A timestamp, familiar voice, or personal detail does not independently settle those questions.

## Rules to settle before a story depends on them

| Open rule | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Formation and persistence of pockets; effects of disconnection | Prevents arbitrary danger when someone repairs or switches off equipment |
| Wired continuity, power, and broken links | Makes the travel exception investigable without becoming a guaranteed route |
| Relative time inside and between pockets | Supports coherent arrival accounts and source chronology |
| Interaction with displaced transmissions | Separates receiving a past recording from contacting its participants or changing events |
| AI access, memory, attention, and ability to alter signals | Prevents an all-knowing intelligence from explaining every clue |
| Human maintenance or other dependence | Gives the intelligence a concrete reason to need people |
| Personal attraction of signals | Keeps emotional relevance consistent without blaming people for anomalies |
| Metaphysical pursuit and eventual disclosure | Shapes the global arc without prematurely selecting its ending |

The themes do not establish a technical prohibition on every possible form of contact with the past. Whether events can be changed is still open. Resolve that rule before writing a consequential cross-time exchange.
