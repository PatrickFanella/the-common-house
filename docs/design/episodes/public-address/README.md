## Signal and change

An ordinary radio logger replays a supposedly private call on the district news feed. The case initially resembles an anomalous leak.

The leak was an ordinary editorial act. Bex can stop spreading the recording, identify her error, and offer a repair Nessa chooses; she cannot purchase forgiveness through a broadcast.

The central act changes an audience, an observable practice, or the way the character participates. It does not score empathy or award an explanation for choosing a kind line.
