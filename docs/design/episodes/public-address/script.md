# Public address: playable script

[Brief](README.md) · [Contract](case-contract.md). Exact lines below are draft dialogue. Present-day play remains inside the house; remote participants appear through bounded radio or text sources. Source playback supplies no unrecorded past scenes.

## Shared presentation contract

Each exchange begins through an explicit interaction. The player keeps their camera; conversations pause movement using the shared modal pattern. Pause stops progression, skip opens the complete labelled transcript, and close returns to play. Only explicit acknowledgment acquires a source. No automatic microphone activation, timed choice, empathy score, or required physical performance. Text-first staging is sufficient for a first implementation. All meaningful sounds and image observations are written below.

## PA-S00: an ordinary interruption

PLAY + INTERACT. Begin in the kitchen or shared repair space. The character's ordinary activity is available as flavor, not a required minigame. Open the exchange explicitly. A persistent receiver lead remains if it is closed or skipped.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| PA-L001 | Bex | The network has been repeating something it should not have. |
| PA-L002 | Min | Repeating, or receiving a copy? |
| PA-L003 | Bex | I would prefer the first one. |

Exit to the receiver by player action. No real-time deadline or mandatory actor walk.

## PA-S01: what actually arrived

INTERACT + MEDIA. Show source origin, stored/live status, and audience before presenting these records. Each is separately replayable and acknowledgeable. A private source's content is not an outgoing payload. A sensitive withheld remainder stays withheld even in transcript mode.

### PA-T01

Stored local radio logger from the original private channel; Nessa consents to the player inspecting only the routing excerpt.

> Nessa: Is this just you?

> Bex: Just me. The news line is closed. Description: logger header names the private channel; sensitive remainder withheld.

### PA-D01

Current news rundown and logger event, already held by Bex; shown to the player with her permission.

> Rundown description: the copied segment has the same file identifier as the private recording. Logger description: it played on the district news channel. Bex: I dragged the wrong line. Then I did not check it.

### PA-C01

Present-day private radio conversation with Nessa, player and Nessa only; explicit invitation required.

> Nessa: Tell them you played something you had promised to keep private. Do not tell them what it was. I do not need an apology with a trailer.

Keep the first two sources independently visible in the comparison reader. Their shared origin or matching field is only the specific support stated in the contract, not universal authentication. The third source may be offered privately; closing its conversation does not acquire it.

## PA-S02: compare, then ask

PLAY + INTERACT. The player chooses the two factual sources at the local reader, then opens a technical or resident conversation. Identify every participant. Deliver the following supported exchange only after the comparison; otherwise offer "We can check the records before guessing" and keep the sources available.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| PA-L004 | Player | The file identifier matches your rundown. |
| PA-L005 | Bex | I know. |
| PA-L006 | Player | Then we can explain the route without playing the call again. |
| PA-L007 | Bex | I was going to include it so people knew what I meant. |

Acknowledgment records PA-E01. It does not authorize any subsequent sharing. The underlying records remain replayable.

## PA-S03: the question behind the evidence

INTERACT + DIRECTED. Private present-day conversation at a quiet table or on a clearly identified private radio channel. Optional personal depth is offered, never a key for unlocking the factual files. Ask "Would you like to talk about it?" before the exchange; "Not now" closes and leaves the lead.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| PA-L008 | Nessa | I do not want to be the reason everyone has a meeting. |
| PA-L009 | Player | What would you like us to do now? |
| PA-L010 | Nessa | Leave my part out. You can tell them what happened to it. |

Participants are Nessa and the player only, on her explicitly agreed private channel. Bex does not hear this account.

Silence and acting are atmosphere. They supply no invisible fact and set no player emotion. Close or skip preserves a complete readable account with separate acknowledgment.

## PA-S04: choose an act

CHOICE. Show the supported factual reveal and its remaining unknowns, then offer:

| Option | Text | Character response |
|---|---|---|
| PA-Q01-A | Help Bex send the scoped public correction | Bex: I played a private recording on the news channel. That was my error. Please do not forward it. I will not repeat its contents here. |
| PA-Q01-B | Keep the correction ready while Bex stops local replay | Nessa: Stop playing it here. I can decide about another announcement tomorrow. |
| PA-Q01-C | Leave this open for now. | Player: We can come back to it. |

Response A proposes PA-O01: Nessa authorizes only the correction text above. Bex separately agrees to send it. Show the district news channel as recipient, confirm, and send once. Unheard corrections remain possible.

Response B proposes PA-O02: Nessa chooses a local stop and postpones a public response. Bex suspends the segment locally. No outbound message and no fabricated consent. This is a complete immediate containment outcome with wider repair unresolved.

After either response, show a separate confirmation with those recipients and content. Confirm commits the named action once; cancel sends nothing and keeps the case open. No source playback or sympathetic expression substitutes for agreement. In any branch with no outbound traffic, state "No external transmission" explicitly.

## PA-S05: the room afterward

AFTERMATH + PLAY. Enter through the next explicit household check-in. Show the selected trace, or a neutral pending-case lead if no outcome was committed. Do not wait for elapsed wall-clock time.

- After PA-O01: A physical two-column rundown marks private and public material. Bex removes the old segment from the house replay list without deleting the evidence record.
- After PA-O02: The affected replay entry shows "withheld at source's request." A written correction remains face down in Bex's tray, not automatically sent later.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| PA-L011 | Bex | I cannot tell whether this reaches everyone who heard it. |
| PA-L012 | Nessa | Then do not say it does. |
| PA-L013 | Bex | I will not. |

The exchange is present-day. Acknowledge the aftermath once. Leaving the room retains it; replay does not repeat a send or rewrite the outcome.

## Carry-forward and staging limits

A public Episode 3 correction may be cited as an example of explicit scope. The private Episode 3 branch permits only a general observation about the local manual.

House policy starts distinguishing retained evidence from available rebroadcast material. This becomes consequential when a later system requests access; Episode 4 supplies a practical boundary, not an AI clue.

No final assets, licensed music, actual electronics procedure, external services, or cinematics package is specified. A later implementation issue must select concrete interaction targets and test the claimed privacy and access, rather than assuming a quiet corner is private.
