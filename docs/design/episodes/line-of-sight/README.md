Sable is remote refuge courier who has stopped volunteering to leave. Sable returned unharmed from an attempted journey, but arrived at a different refuge. Others call the trip a success because she survived. She wants the place she left to count as a loss.

She organizes a radio chess tournament using improvised boards and routinely forgets which colour she is. She is good at arranging rematches.

## Signal and change

Physical continuity is a useful lead, with a documented break and limited trials. Sable may help people describe a route without promising to walk it or calling her displacement harmless.
