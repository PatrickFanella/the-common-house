# Line of sight: facts and branches

[Brief](README.md) · [Script](script.md). IDs below are authoring references, not Unity APIs.

## Author truth and chronology

Sable's original attempted return followed an overhead line until it ended at a demolished junction. The contemporary trial uses a different continuous service run and remains within prepared contact points. Two supervised crossings have maintained radio sequence and arrival landmarks; one earlier attempt near a break did not. These are bounded reports. They do not prove reliable travel over unknown distance or a route to Sable's old refuge.

The original events described in stored sources precede their arrival here. Present-day accounts follow local receipt and inspection. Original device labels, receipt order, author chronology, and character interpretation are separate. Stored sources replay identically. Contemporary replies are to contemporary people or the explicitly identified current endpoint; no participant in a stored recording is contacted through playback.

The exact reveal is: Physical continuity is a useful lead, with a documented break and limited trials. Sable may help people describe a route without promising to walk it or calling her displacement harmless.

Remain unknown: Whether the correlation generalizes, how underground sections can be traced, and whether Sable's previous refuge is reachable. No one has found a universal navigation instrument.

## Acquisition, comparison, and eligibility

Explicit acknowledgment records sources once. The player compares the first two sources, with both descriptions visible, to mark `LS-E01`, the supported factual comparison. The source table states its inference limits. The third source is separately attributed testimony or a contemporary report, not an automatic notebook conclusion. Personal account acquisition records no sharing permission.

Scenes `LS-S00` through `LS-S05` remain reachable by persistent local leads. The final action requires the first two sources and the completed comparison, followed by the relevant current agreement. Where the first two records already suffice, the personal account stays optional. In Line of sight, this means the player can inspect the factual record without obtaining a confession. The first two sources alone never authorize publishing private content.

For every outgoing or shared act, show the actual recipients, exact included content, and whose authorization covers it. Authorizations in source text apply only to their stated material. A new confirmation is required for the present action. If an authorized character postpones an act, the source remains available and no automated later send occurs.
