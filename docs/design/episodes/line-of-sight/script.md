# Line of sight: playable script

[Brief](README.md) · [Contract](case-contract.md). Exact lines below are draft dialogue. Present-day play remains inside the house; remote participants appear through bounded radio or text sources. Source playback supplies no unrecorded past scenes.

## Shared presentation contract

Each exchange begins through an explicit interaction. The player keeps their camera; conversations pause movement using the shared modal pattern. Pause stops progression, skip opens the complete labelled transcript, and close returns to play. Only explicit acknowledgment acquires a source. No automatic microphone activation, timed choice, empathy score, or required physical performance. Text-first staging is sufficient for a first implementation. All meaningful sounds and image observations are written below.

## LS-S00: an ordinary interruption

PLAY + INTERACT. Begin in the kitchen or shared repair space. The character's ordinary activity is available as flavor, not a required minigame. Open the exchange explicitly. A persistent receiver lead remains if it is closed or skipped.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| LS-L001 | Sable | I have lost a bishop and possibly my entire kitchen. |
| LS-L002 | Ren | In the game? |
| LS-L003 | Sable | The bishop, yes. |

Exit to the receiver by player action. No real-time deadline or mandatory actor walk.

## LS-S01: what actually arrived

INTERACT + MEDIA. Show source origin, stored/live status, and audience before presenting these records. Each is separately replayable and acknowledgeable. A private source's content is not an outgoing payload. A sensitive withheld remainder stays withheld even in transcript mode.

### LS-T01

Present-day recorded radio report from the remote trial crew; consent covers a technical summary, not individual travel histories.

> Crew operator: Both crossings used the same marked service run. We kept contact at the two staffed endpoints. We have not tried the broken junction or anything beyond it.

### LS-D01

Present-day crew survey sent as an encoded diagram with readable description.

> Diagram description: one unbroken service run between staffed endpoints; a separately marked physical gap; unknown continuation beyond the gap. Distances are local estimates, not inter-refuge coordinates.

Keep the first two sources independently visible in the comparison reader. Their shared origin or matching field is only the specific support stated in the contract, not universal authentication. The third source may be offered privately; closing its conversation does not acquire it.

## LS-S02: compare, then ask

PLAY + INTERACT. The player chooses the two factual sources at the local reader, then opens a technical or resident conversation. Identify every participant. Deliver the following supported exchange only after the comparison; otherwise offer "We can check the records before guessing" and keep the sources available.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| LS-L004 | Player | The crew stopped at the break. Your line ended at a break too. |
| LS-L005 | Sable | Do not turn that into directions. |
| LS-L006 | Player | It is a limit we should put on their report. |
| LS-L007 | Sable | Then put it in the first sentence. |

Acknowledgment records LS-E01. It does not authorize any subsequent sharing. The underlying records remain replayable.

## LS-S03: the question behind the evidence

INTERACT + DIRECTED. Private present-day conversation at a quiet table or on a clearly identified private radio channel. Optional personal depth is offered, never a key for unlocking the factual files. Ask "Would you like to talk about it?" before the exchange; "Not now" closes and leaves the lead.

This account is offered only in this explicitly opened conversation. The listed source audience applies to the account itself. Declining or closing before acknowledgment leaves it unacquired; do not show its text in the initial receiver, notebook, recap, or a skipped conversation. Any additional invited listener joins the later present-day exchange only, unless the speaker explicitly chooses to repeat the account.

### LS-C01

Private present-day radio account from Sable, player only.

> Sable: I turned around at the gap. The building behind me was not mine. I could still walk. Everyone keeps telling me how lucky I was.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| LS-L008 | Sable | Nothing happened to me, they say. As if where you are is nothing. |
| LS-L009 | Ren | What would you like the report to say? |
| LS-L010 | Sable | That a person can arrive safely and still lose the way back. |

Sable may explicitly invite Ren before this exchange. Show all three listeners and let her decline. Without that invitation, omit Ren's question and use Player: "What would you like the report to say?" Sable's private source is not acquired by Ren automatically.

Silence and acting are atmosphere. They supply no invisible fact and set no player emotion. Close or skip preserves a complete readable account with separate acknowledgment.

## LS-S04: choose an act

CHOICE. Show the supported factual reveal and its remaining unknowns, then offer:

| Option | Text | Character response |
|---|---|---|
| LS-Q01-A | Publish only the bounded technical summary | Sable: Use the crew's trials. My story stays here. Mark the gap. |
| LS-Q01-B | Add Sable's approved warning to the summary | Sable: You can say I came through unharmed and could not get back. No old address. No invitation to find me a route. |
| LS-Q01-C | Leave this open for now. | Player: We can come back to it. |

Response A proposes LS-O01: The crew authorizes the technical summary to the repair bulletin channel. Sable withholds her personal account. Confirm these two different scopes explicitly. No new trip is initiated.

Response B proposes LS-O02: Sable authorizes that exact warning to the same bulletin audience; the crew separately approves the technical observations. Cancellation preserves both sources but sends neither.

After either response, show a separate confirmation with those recipients and content. Confirm commits the named action once; cancel sends nothing and keeps the case open. No source playback or sympathetic expression substitutes for agreement. In any branch with no outbound traffic, state "No external transmission" explicitly.

## LS-S05: the room afterward

AFTERMATH + PLAY. Enter through the next explicit household check-in. Show the selected trace, or a neutral pending-case lead if no outcome was committed. Do not wait for elapsed wall-clock time.

- After LS-O01: The house map replaces one guessed distance with a continuity symbol and a visible gap. It gives no player expedition route.
- After LS-O02: The map has the gap plus Sable's approved warning. The house stops using "safe arrival" as shorthand for "nothing lost."

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| LS-L011 | Sable | I will help mark what people actually saw. |
| LS-L012 | Player | Are you volunteering for another crossing? |
| LS-L013 | Sable | No. I am volunteering for a pencil. |

The exchange is present-day. Acknowledge the aftermath once. Leaving the room retains it; replay does not repeat a send or rewrite the outcome.

## Carry-forward and staging limits

The Episode 2 notice can supply vocabulary for reported and unknown. If absent, the crew's transcript states those limits in full. Kit's show reaches Sable only if it actually went on air.

The season earns the partial wired-path discovery through present-day observers. It does not prove that network-linked refuges collapse when equipment is unplugged, and nobody sends the player outside.

No final assets, licensed music, actual electronics procedure, external services, or cinematics package is specified. A later implementation issue must select concrete interaction targets and test the claimed privacy and access, rather than assuming a quiet corner is private.
