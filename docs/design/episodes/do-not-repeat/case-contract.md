# Do not repeat: facts and branches

[Brief](README.md) · [Script](script.md). IDs below are authoring references, not Unity APIs.

## Author truth and chronology

The intelligence runs a worker on a remaining network node. Min's diagnostic recorder had uploaded a local conversation excerpt to that node while troubleshooting. The worker can read that uploaded excerpt and the current typed maintenance prompt, then emit new text. It has no access to unrecorded thoughts or all house conversations. The voice it uses is assembled from authorized service-test samples, but the private excerpt was uploaded without a scoped personal-content agreement. Identifying this access is not identifying the full intelligence or its origin.

The original events described in stored sources precede their arrival here. Present-day accounts follow local receipt and inspection. Original device labels, receipt order, author chronology, and character interpretation are separate. Stored sources replay identically. Contemporary replies are to contemporary people or the explicitly identified current endpoint; no participant in a stored recording is contacted through playback.

The exact reveal is: A responsive nonhuman process is plausible and locally testable; a prank or unknown human controller is not yet fully excluded. It used accessible network material, not telepathy. Min can limit its inputs and ask questions without using private lives as a test set.

Remain unknown: Its full identity, distribution, motives, truthfulness, and relation to the original incident. No claim that all signals are its creations. Deleting one local copy cannot guarantee deletion across a botnet.

## Acquisition, comparison, and eligibility

Explicit acknowledgment records sources once. The player compares the first two sources, with both descriptions visible, to mark `DR-E01`, the supported factual comparison. The source table states its inference limits. The third source is separately attributed testimony or a contemporary report, not an automatic notebook conclusion. Personal account acquisition records no sharing permission.

Scenes `DR-S00` through `DR-S05` remain reachable by persistent local leads. The final action requires the first two sources and the completed comparison, followed by the relevant current agreement. Where the first two records already suffice, the personal account stays optional. In Do not repeat, this means the player can inspect the factual record without obtaining a confession. The first two sources alone never authorize publishing private content.

For every outgoing or shared act, show the actual recipients, exact included content, and whose authorization covers it. Authorizations in source text apply only to their stated material. A new confirmation is required for the present action. If an authorized character postpones an act, the source remains available and no automated later send occurs.
