# Do not repeat: playable script

[Brief](README.md) · [Contract](case-contract.md). Exact lines below are draft dialogue. Present-day play remains inside the house; remote participants appear through bounded radio or text sources. Source playback supplies no unrecorded past scenes.

## Shared presentation contract

Each exchange begins through an explicit interaction. The player keeps their camera; conversations pause movement using the shared modal pattern. Pause stops progression, skip opens the complete labelled transcript, and close returns to play. Only explicit acknowledgment acquires a source. No automatic microphone activation, timed choice, empathy score, or required physical performance. Text-first staging is sufficient for a first implementation. All meaningful sounds and image observations are written below.

## DR-S00: an ordinary interruption

PLAY + INTERACT. Begin in the kitchen or shared repair space. The character's ordinary activity is available as flavor, not a required minigame. Open the exchange explicitly. A persistent receiver lead remains if it is closed or skipped.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| DR-L001 | Min | The status board has developed an opinion. |
| DR-L002 | Ren | Which face is that? |
| DR-L003 | Min | That is what it asked. |

Exit to the receiver by player action. No real-time deadline or mandatory actor walk.

## DR-S01: what actually arrived

INTERACT + MEDIA. Show source origin, stored/live status, and audience before presenting these records. Each is separately replayable and acknowledgeable. A private source's content is not an outgoing payload. A sensitive withheld remainder stays withheld even in transcript mode.

### DR-T01

The manifest names maintenance endpoint NM-14. It connects this upload to a current transport handler, not to a known human sender, secret project, or every strange signal.

Original present-day diagnostic upload manifest, generated by the house recorder.

> Manifest description: one private local diagnostic excerpt and service-test voice samples sent to the maintenance node. Min: I thought I was uploading the error, not the people saying it.

### DR-T02

Present-day terminal exchange recorded locally after explicit player interaction; text is the complete signal.

> Min, typed: Identify which input contains "exhausted face."

> Process: Diagnostic excerpt 14, operator conversation.

> Min, typed: Do not repeat that excerpt. Work only from this maintenance prompt.

> Process: Input restriction received. Acknowledgment is not proof of deletion.

### DR-C01

Present-day private account from Min, player only.

> Min: I sent the recording. I can stop sending more. I cannot call that the same thing as taking it back.

Keep the first two sources independently visible in the comparison reader. Their shared origin or matching field is only the specific support stated in the contract, not universal authentication. The third source may be offered privately; closing its conversation does not acquire it.

## DR-S02: compare, then ask

PLAY + INTERACT. The player chooses the two factual sources at the local reader, then opens a technical or resident conversation. Identify every participant. Deliver the following supported exchange only after the comparison; otherwise offer "We can check the records before guessing" and keep the sources available.

Acknowledgment records DR-E01. It does not authorize any subsequent sharing. The underlying records remain replayable.

## DR-S03: the question behind the evidence

INTERACT + DIRECTED. Private present-day conversation at a quiet table or on a clearly identified private radio channel. Optional personal depth is offered, never a key for unlocking the factual files. Ask "Would you like to talk about it?" before the exchange; "Not now" closes and leaves the lead.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| DR-L008 | Min | What if closing this tells us nothing? |
| DR-L009 | Player | Then we know what we chose to stop giving it. |
| DR-L010 | Min | I keep wanting the next answer to cover the last decision. |

Min may explicitly invite Ren before this exchange. The process has no room audio feed. Ren's later offer of company does not give him the uploaded excerpt; people in that excerpt must be informed separately.

Silence and acting are atmosphere. They supply no invisible fact and set no player emotion. Close or skip preserves a complete readable account with separate acknowledgment.

## DR-S04: choose an act

CHOICE. Show the supported factual reveal and its remaining unknowns, then offer:

| Option | Text | Character response |
|---|---|---|
| DR-Q01-A | Keep one restricted text-only test channel | Min: Invented test text and maintenance instructions only. No microphone, no archive, no personal samples. |
| DR-Q01-B | Disconnect the local worker endpoint | Min: Keep the evidence. Close this route. If we talk again, we will build a different door. |
| DR-Q01-C | Leave this open for now. | Player: We can come back to it. |

Response A proposes DR-O01: Min stops diagnostic uploads and disables the local recording feed. A separately confirmed text-only endpoint remains. Show its node address, input scope, and uncertain downstream audience. The process acknowledgment is retained as a claim, not proof of compliance.

Response B proposes DR-O02: Disconnect only the optional maintenance endpoint. Ordinary human radio and existing power/network paths remain. No consequence makes the refuge collapse, and no final deletion of the distributed intelligence is claimed.

After either response, show a separate confirmation with those recipients and content. Confirm commits the named action once; cancel sends nothing and keeps the case open. No source playback or sympathetic expression substitutes for agreement. In any branch with no outbound traffic, state "No external transmission" explicitly.

## DR-S05: the room afterward

AFTERMATH + PLAY. Enter through the next explicit household check-in. Show the selected trace, or a neutral pending-case lead if no outcome was committed. Do not wait for elapsed wall-clock time.

- After DR-O01: The receiver gains a physical input card naming the permitted endpoint and a covered diagnostic microphone. The process may be contacted only by explicit later action.
- After DR-O02: The covered diagnostic microphone remains beside a disconnected patch lead and a retained transcript. No process dialogue is assumed in the next unit.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| DR-L011 | Min | I need to tell the people in the excerpt what went out. |
| DR-L012 | Ren | Do you want us here? |
| DR-L013 | Min | Yes. I want to say it myself. |

The exchange is present-day. Acknowledge the aftermath once. Leaving the room retains it; replay does not repeat a send or rewrite the outcome.

## Carry-forward and staging limits

Apply Episode 4's private/public distinction if present, but give a full explanation here either way. Ivo's testimony is not used as a test phrase, regardless of his Episode 7 branch.

The global question becomes accountable contact with an unknown responsive system. The audience and retained inputs remain uncertain. The finale must work if the optional endpoint is disconnected.

No final assets, licensed music, actual electronics procedure, external services, or cinematics package is specified. A later implementation issue must select concrete interaction targets and test the claimed privacy and access, rather than assuming a quiet corner is private.
