# Held calls: facts and branches

[Brief](README.md) · [Script](script.md). IDs below are authoring references, not Unity APIs.

## Author truth and chronology

Ivo and another operator, Tess, manually triaged an overloaded line during the first disruption. Ivo diverted a staff channel to check on one known employee while callers remained queued. Tess challenged the priority, then used a remaining line for the oldest waiting call. Some calls ended without contact. A later partial queue snapshot cannot identify why every call ended or which caller survived. Ivo did prefer a known person once; he did not cause the incident or possess a secret cure.

The original events described in stored sources precede their arrival here. Present-day accounts follow local receipt and inspection. Original device labels, receipt order, author chronology, and character interpretation are separate. Stored sources replay identically. Contemporary replies are to contemporary people or the explicitly identified current endpoint; no participant in a stored recording is contacted through playback.

The exact reveal is: The record supports a specific unequal choice and a coworker's corrective act. It does not support either complete vindication or a total accounting of the missing. The house can document that choice without treating shelter as a reward for a clean past.

Remain unknown: Tess's present fate, the outcomes of most callers, and the precise timing of disconnection. No secret quantum-project evidence is found in an ordinary office queue.

## Acquisition, comparison, and eligibility

Explicit acknowledgment records sources once. The player compares the first two sources, with both descriptions visible, to mark `HC-E01`, the supported factual comparison. The source table states its inference limits. The third source is separately attributed testimony or a contemporary report, not an automatic notebook conclusion. Personal account acquisition records no sharing permission.

Scenes `HC-S00` through `HC-S05` remain reachable by persistent local leads. The final action requires the first two sources and the completed comparison, followed by the relevant current agreement. Where the first two records already suffice, the personal account stays optional. In Held calls, this means the player can inspect the factual record without obtaining a confession. The first two sources alone never authorize publishing private content.

For every outgoing or shared act, show the actual recipients, exact included content, and whose authorization covers it. Authorizations in source text apply only to their stated material. A new confirmation is required for the present action. If an authorized character postpones an act, the source remains available and no automated later send occurs.
