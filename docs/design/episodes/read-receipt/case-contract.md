Remain unknown: The collaborator's real identity, current location, current wishes, and present fate. Historical permission to use a handle is not permission to reveal their identity.

## Acquisition, comparison, and eligibility

Explicit acknowledgment records sources once. The player compares the first two sources, with both descriptions visible, to mark `RR-E01`, the supported factual comparison. The source table states its inference limits. The third source is separately attributed testimony or a contemporary report, not an automatic notebook conclusion. Personal account acquisition records no sharing permission.

Scenes `RR-S00` through `RR-S05` remain reachable by persistent local leads. The final action requires the first two sources and the completed comparison, followed by the relevant current agreement. Where the first two records already suffice, the personal account stays optional. In Read receipt, this means the player can inspect the factual record without obtaining a confession. The first two sources alone never authorize publishing private content.

For every outgoing or shared act, show the actual recipients, exact included content, and whose authorization covers it. Authorizations in source text apply only to their stated material. A new confirmation is required for the present action. If an authorized character postpones an act, the source remains available and no automated later send occurs.
