## Draft authority and emotional core

Drafted under the owner's 2026-09-29 permission to make creative decisions. This is a complete text-first story treatment for review, not a final cast, implemented episode, release promise, or new world rule. Stable namespace: `RR`.

Dev is house repair volunteer and former bulletin-board organizer. Dev built a useful tool and let everyone believe he built it alone. He fears that correcting the story will erase the part he really did contribute.

He repairs a temperamental label printer and produces increasingly insulting labels for his own tools. His first success is a label reading "working, allegedly."

## Signal and change

A displaced AIM-style chat export and a mailed patch receipt identify the person whose work Dev once passed off as his own.

Dev can give an accurate account of shared work without deleting his contribution. Delivery evidence does not establish what latchkey currently wants, where they are, or whether they survived.

The central act changes an audience, an observable practice, or the way the character participates. It does not score empathy or award an explanation for choosing a kind line.

## Domestic aftermath

- `RR-O01`: The tool shelf gains a card naming both contributions. Dev starts asking for collaborators before announcing a repair.
- `RR-O02`: The local manual has an added credit card. Dev leaves a blank space on the next repair sheet for another contributor.
