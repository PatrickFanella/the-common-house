## Purpose and emotional movement

The newcomer reaches a converted telecommunications office, receives shelter, learns why walking back is unsafe, and hears the society connected by radio. Initial relief develops into an understanding that refuge and confinement occupy the same building. Household care remains sincere.

By completion, the player should have grounds to understand that this building is inhabited and offers shelter; conventional travel between refuges is unreliable; radio connects isolated communities; and the residents have adapted to months of disruption without understanding it fully.

## Proposed arrival treatment

The player expects a wrong turn to be reversible. Dialogue permits uncertainty or a frightening journey too; it imposes no former job, family, destination, precise elapsed time, or personal connection to the incident. The player's actual pre-arrival experience remains open under W-06.

Start on the entrance frontage already within the refuge, with a short readable route to the door. This is arrival staging at the outpost, not an expedition through the intervening areas. Exact exterior geometry needs scene review. If the current scene cannot support it independently of active work, the opening may begin at the threshold with the outside visible through the door.

## Working cast and presentation

Ren welcomes the player and Ivo tends the radio desk, using existing provisional names and functions. Their detailed lines remain draft treatment. Background residents and a Jo cameo are optional later additions. No new character or final asset is required to read or prototype the intro.

Proposed duration is about ten minutes before a case, with freedom to linger. No timing gate expires food, shelter, information, or a broadcast. Present-day exchanges can begin as readable text and later receive acting. Essential information is never carried only by an animation.

## Opening anomaly

A displaced office-closed telephone greeting names the former telecommunications company. It is a concrete recorded source with an uncertain arrival path. An operator recognizes it; its actual device/archive origin is open. The greeting does not establish an AI, ghost, current caller, or two-way link. This remains a proposed closing beat and may be revised during review.

## Boundary and handoff

The intro ends with food, a place to sleep, and an offer to learn a later shift. The first episode is not assigned. Case 01 remains the separate POC baseline. An explicit later shift-start action can bridge to the chosen Episode 1; no case begins merely because the player closes a panel.
