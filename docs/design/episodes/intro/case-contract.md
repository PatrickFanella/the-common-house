# Intro: learning and scene contract

[Package guide](README.md) · [Script](script.md). This is an orientation contract, not a mystery with scored endings. Core setting follows the confirmed world; exact dialogue, staging, and anomaly remain draft treatment.

## Truth and information boundaries

The player has arrived at the telecommunications refuge after being caught in the disrupted areas. Their subjective journey, biography, and elapsed time are unresolved. The wrong-turn expectation is a playable treatment, not a compulsory author fact.

Residents have experienced months since the incident and increasingly rare arrivals. They offer shelter, know travel is unreliable, and participate in radio society. Neither Ren nor Ivo knows the surviving AI exists. They cannot establish why the pockets are safe. Infrastructure-following travel and the AI are later discoveries, not intro explanations.

The old telephone greeting was generated as a real fictional recorded message. Its original storage/device and displaced transport route remain open. Its concrete source type is sufficient for this small beat; it cannot establish a sender, date, or present caller. No real service is contacted.

## Learning and sources

| Objective | Supports | Acquisition and recovery | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| The building offers shelter | IN-S01, IN-S03, IN-S05 | Explicit shelter interaction; repeated welcome and bedding offer | No forced gratitude, biography, or task |
| Return between pockets is unreliable | IN-C01, threshold account | Acknowledge warning; repeat at reception | Testimony does not reveal all world rules |
| A few months have passed; arrivals are rare | IN-C02, Ivo's account | Optional questions, persistent orientation summary | Does not fix player elapsed time or exact calendar |
| Radio links a diverse population | IN-T01–IN-T04, IN-C03, IN-D01 | Accessible program sequence and desk explanation | Reports remain attributed; distance unknown |
| Ordinary life coexists with anomalous reception | IN-T05, IN-C04 | Recorded greeting and Ivo's recognition | No inferred AI or supernatural identity |

An orientation summary states only learned source-backed information. Full intro completion requires acknowledgment of the shelter offer, warning, network explanation, and opportunity to review the anomaly. Chronology and program details can be delivered through the summary if optional questions were skipped. Closing a scene early leaves its acknowledgment pending; completion does not retroactively mark every optional source acquired.

## State and choices

Track opening scene progress, explicitly acquired observations/accounts, warning/network/shelter acknowledgments, optional preferred address, greeting reviewed, and intro completion. Expression choices have line responses but do not set morality or relationship scores. Store no forced family history, destination, trauma, injury, companion, or exact journey.

IN-Q01 describes arrival; IN-Q02 expresses the player's response to shelter; final options express willingness to learn later. All fold back to hospitality. No choice silently gives permission to broadcast the player's identity.

For the present-day radio exchange, the microphone status is visible and textual; only the generic arrival statement is sent. Device playback is not a live outgoing call. After completion, the desk and records remain available. A new case starts separately, once its connector is authored.

## Play boundaries and cinematic contract

Arrival frontage belongs to outpost staging under D-13. No playable expedition, disappearing route, rescue teleport, death, or lost-area navigation subsystem is introduced. Exact physical boundary and exterior geometry need review before integration. A threshold-only opening is the safe text-first scene alternative.

All directed exchanges follow explicit interaction. Player camera remains under their control; pausing stops delivery. Skip opens full readable lines and requires acknowledgment before applying learning. Close returns to play with pending content recoverable. No one receives information merely because an actor starts talking outside the player's view.

Source transcripts include all words, speaker labels, radio microphone status, and meaningful sounds. Descriptions cover the map and opening environment; acting reactions are atmospheric only. Essential learning works with audio muted and without decoding handwriting or recognizing a voice.

## Routes to verify when implemented

| Route | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| Full orientation | Arrival → warning → reception → broadcasts → meal/shelter → greeting → explicit finish; all core objectives available |
| Minimal spoken questions | Choose privacy/uncertainty, skip optional questions, use persistent summary; same shelter and core knowledge, no assigned name |
| Flexible room order | Meal before desk/reception; return for missing warning/network acknowledgment; no locked essential lead |
| Pause/close/skip | Pause mid-exchange, close early, reopen; acknowledgment neither fabricated nor repeated |
| Replay greeting | Identical stored source; no live call, new clue, or repeated case progression |
| Transcript-only | Same essential information and choices with audio/animation unavailable |
| Pre/post-completion reload | Same learned facts and optional address; no automatic case start or duplicated broadcast |

Human review should establish whether the player feels welcomed, understands why ordinary return is unsafe, recognizes a larger radio society, and distinguishes unknown origin from AI knowledge. Those are observation questions, not unit-test assertions.

## Deferred review details

Choose the former company name, physical frontage boundary, sleeping spot, cast size/cameos, precise performance pacing, and Episode 1 connector during review. These do not prevent reading the complete intro draft. They do prevent an agent from asserting final geometry, branding, or a new case sequence without a scoped decision.
