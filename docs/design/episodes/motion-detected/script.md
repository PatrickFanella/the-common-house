## MD-S00: the breakfast repair

**PLAY.** Kitchen. Jo is repairing a loose connection on the household radio. The player's starting goal is ordinary contact with the household. A mug and a breakfast place are available; eating, picking up the mug, or doing a repair is not required to proceed.

**Environment direction.** A gardening program intermittently plays. A butter knife rests beside the open radio casing. The repair is characterization, not a required electronics puzzle. Retain safe readability and existing product controls when implemented.

| MD-L004 | Jo | Everything broken is apparently mine. |

**DIRECTED, optional.** Deliver the exchange after the player explicitly begins the opening interaction. Jo puts down the tool and heads toward reception. Keep a readable exchange record. Leaving, pausing, or skipping does not lose the lead. A persistent receiver prompt allows MD-S01 even if the exchange is not watched. Actor movement can be omitted in a text-first slice.

**Exit.** Player chooses to inspect the reception receiver. No global time or missed-meal counter advances.

## MD-S01: motion detected

**INTERACT + MEDIA.** Reception/radio desk. Min has decoded a received packet onto a monitor. Before opening the source, show that Jo and Min are present. The initial excerpt contains no confession or identifiable person.

MD-V01 is an eight-second network-camera clip. It looks down concrete basement steps toward a partly framed door. A drain contains leaves. Rain is audible. A bin lid scrapes near the frame edge. A pale shape briefly appears at the top edge, too little to identify. The source overlay reads `MOTION DETECTED`; its calendar date precedes the incident. The date is an asserted device timestamp, not verified chronology.

## MD-S04: the door

**MEDIA + COMPARE.** MD-V02 records a person at the bottom of the steps in a clear enough view for Jo to identify her son. He addresses the camera: "Mum? I know I said you wouldn't have to worry. I left my charger." MD-V03 is the overlapping continuation of the same camera event, recording the same coat and person opening the basement door and stepping through. The image ends without showing the interior. Jo identifies him in MD-C03; the notebook initially labels the person as unidentified.

After MD-L065, add MD-L069, Jo: "And he says it. What he said when he left. He came back." The audio reference to the departure and the uninterrupted camera-event continuation anchor the return; the handrail corroborates the visit period.

MD-L064 is eligible only after MD-C02 and explicit comparison of MD-V02 with MD-V03. Otherwise offer MD-L068: "We can compare it with what you remember. I don't want to guess from the clock." MD-L065 adds Jo's attribution to the repair timing; it does not alone establish a universal cross-time rule.

**Choice.** Offer "Watch it again" and "Leave it paused." Both change presentation only. No new clip appears because the player chooses a sympathetic line. The full return inference and its limits are defined in the contract; earlier visits remain a hypothesis until the authored comparison is complete.

**Exit.** Replay has no emotional or mechanical completion effect. Investigation may continue or the player may offer the closing conversation.
