## Camera and transport boundaries

Working source is an internet-connected security camera above the back basement steps, with a microphone covering the entrance. Clips show only the exterior frame and carry sounds within microphone range. They never show the basement couch, bags, Jo's private actions, or unrecorded past dialogue.

The outpost receives encoded image/audio packets over its fictional radio network and renders locally reconstructed clips on suitable equipment. This is a new authored presentation capability, not an existing Unity video implementation. All input is local fictional content. No live network-camera or internet integration is needed.

For this episode the fragments are fixed recordings and interaction with their past participants is unavailable. Replay is identical. Their route through displaced time is not fully explained. AI involvement is undetermined and unnecessary for completion; the agent must not add an AI identity, message, or fabricated sender.

Clip order is authored privately. Device time and receipt time remain separate. MD-V02 and MD-V03 have an overlapping segment of the same continuous event, with matched movement, camera view, sound, and no hidden temporal jump at the join. Their reconstructable continuity supports a single entry sequence. A shared device label alone does not prove authenticity; present this evidence as the available record plus Jo's recognition, not infallible digital forensics.

## Recovery and cinematic behavior

Directed moments start after explicit interaction. Pause, skip, and close are available. Skipping offers the complete readable content before an explicit acknowledgment applies acquisition; closing early grants nothing not yet acknowledged. No critical clue exists only in acting, a camera cut, lip reading, or an uncaptioned sound.

Evidence remains replayable before and after resolution. Replays do not duplicate consent, invite Min, transmit material, or advance the household phase. After resolution, new consequential choices are disabled, but sources already available remain readable. A closed-case replay cannot silently create an unrecorded disclosure.

Use an episode-specific disposable save namespace in tests. Loading an MD save as Case 01 or vice versa must fail visibly and preserve the file. Persist line progress, acquired sources, permissions, outcome, audience, and aftermath. No background clock changes state during pause or deferral.
