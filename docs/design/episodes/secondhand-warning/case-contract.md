[Brief](README.md) · [Script](script.md). IDs below are authoring references, not Unity APIs.

## Author truth and chronology

Before the incident, Mara recorded a warning about a temporary bypass on Pump B. That bypass was later removed. The displaced recording is genuine, but describes an earlier configuration. The current restart still needs an ordinary inspection; proving the warning is old does not prove the equipment safe.

The original events described in stored sources precede their arrival here. Present-day accounts follow local receipt and inspection. Original device labels, receipt order, author chronology, and character interpretation are separate. Stored sources replay identically. Contemporary replies are to contemporary people or the explicitly identified current endpoint; no participant in a stored recording is contacted through playback.

The exact reveal is: The warning concerns a removed bypass, not a forecast. Mara can admit uncertainty and arrange a present-day check without surrendering responsibility.

Remain unknown: The complete age of the displaced recording and its route to the house; whether any future restart will succeed. No medical or engineering procedure is taught by this case.

## Acquisition, comparison, and eligibility

Explicit acknowledgment records sources once. The player compares the first two sources, with both descriptions visible, to mark `SW-E01`, the supported factual comparison. The source table states its inference limits. The third source is separately attributed testimony or a contemporary report, not an automatic notebook conclusion. Personal account acquisition records no sharing permission.

Scenes `SW-S00` through `SW-S05` remain reachable by persistent local leads. The final action requires the first two sources and the completed comparison, followed by the relevant current agreement. Where the first two records already suffice, the personal account stays optional. In Secondhand warning, this means the player can inspect the factual record without obtaining a confession. The first two sources alone never authorize publishing private content.

For every outgoing or shared act, show the actual recipients, exact included content, and whose authorization covers it. Authorizations in source text apply only to their stated material. A new confirmation is required for the present action. If an authorized character postpones an act, the source remains available and no automated later send occurs.
