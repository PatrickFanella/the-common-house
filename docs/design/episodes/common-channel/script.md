# Common channel: playable script

[Brief](README.md) · [Contract](case-contract.md). Exact lines below are draft dialogue. Present-day play remains inside the house; remote participants appear through bounded radio or text sources. Source playback supplies no unrecorded past scenes.

## Shared presentation contract

Each exchange begins through an explicit interaction. The player keeps their camera; conversations pause movement using the shared modal pattern. Pause stops progression, skip opens the complete labelled transcript, and close returns to play. Only explicit acknowledgment acquires a source. No automatic microphone activation, timed choice, empathy score, or required physical performance. Text-first staging is sufficient for a first implementation. All meaningful sounds and image observations are written below.

## CC-S00: an ordinary interruption

PLAY + INTERACT. Begin in the kitchen or shared repair space. The character's ordinary activity is available as flavor, not a required minigame. Open the exchange explicitly. A persistent receiver lead remains if it is closed or skipped.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| CC-L001 | Kit | Five seconds. Name the station, say hello, stop. |
| CC-L002 | Jo | I can do two of those. |
| CC-L003 | Ivo | Nobody has ever done the third. |

Exit to the receiver by player action. No real-time deadline or mandatory actor walk.

## CC-S01: what actually arrived

INTERACT + MEDIA. Show source origin, stored/live status, and audience before presenting these records. Each is separately replayable and acknowledgeable. A private source's content is not an outgoing payload. A sensitive withheld remainder stays withheld even in transcript mode.

### CC-D01

Present-day crew inspection ledger and voluntary participation replies, all sent on the coordination channel.

> Crew: We approve reports from the staffed endpoints only. No person is crossing the uninspected leg. Orchard: We agree to the contact test, not to publish household names.

### CC-T01

Orchard forwards the proposal through ordinary current radio as an encoded document. Its outer envelope names NM-14. Receiving and inspecting it does not reconnect the house's optional maintenance endpoint. Any reply to that endpoint is a new, separately confirmed connection.

Current maintenance proposal received at the shared desk; available after an explicit import even if the Episode 8 endpoint was disconnected.

> Proposal description: inspected local links plus a continuation marked complete in an old service table. Min: The continuation is a record, not today's cable. We can inspect the proposal without opening a reply path.

Keep the first two sources independently visible in the comparison reader. Their shared origin or matching field is only the specific support stated in the contract, not universal authentication. The third source may be offered privately; closing its conversation does not acquire it.

PLAY + INTERACT. The player chooses the two factual sources at the local reader, then opens a technical or resident conversation. Identify every participant. Deliver the following supported exchange only after the comparison; otherwise offer "We can check the records before guessing" and keep the sources available.

## CC-S03: the question behind the evidence

INTERACT + DIRECTED. Private present-day conversation at a quiet table or on a clearly identified private radio channel. Optional personal depth is offered, never a key for unlocking the factual files. Ask "Would you like to talk about it?" before the exchange; "Not now" closes and leaves the lead.

| Line | Speaker | Text |
|---|---|---|
| CC-L008 | Ivo | What do we tell the places that cannot join? |
| CC-L009 | Ren | That this is what we checked. Not the border of who matters. |
| CC-L010 | Jo | Leave room in the ledger. There will be more people than lines. |

This is the agreed coordination meeting. Participants are the player, Ren, Ivo, Jo, and Min; private case accounts remain excluded. The process is not listening to the room. Any process reply is a separate explicit typed action in S04.

Silence and acting are atmosphere. They supply no invisible fact and set no player emotion. Close or skip preserves a complete readable account with separate acknowledgment.

## CC-S04: choose an act

CHOICE. Show the supported factual reveal and its remaining unknowns, then offer:

| Option | Text | Character response |
|---|---|---|
| CC-Q01-A | Approve the bounded human-led test with a restricted process reply | Min, typed: Inspected endpoints only. No household histories. You may propose a check, not authorize a crossing. / Process: Records cannot tell me whether a person can return. Your observations change the model. I cannot make the absent leg continuous. |
| CC-Q01-B | Approve the bounded test using human radio only | Ren: We can read a proposal without giving its sender a place at the table. Keep the inspected links. Let the people at each end answer for them. |
| CC-Q01-C | Leave this open for now. | Player: We can come back to it. |

Response A proposes CC-O01: All named participating operators approve their own contact test. The house separately confirms the new text-only process channel, including after an Episode 8 disconnect. No presumed AI compliance and no personal archive upload.

Response B proposes CC-O02: All participating operators approve the same limited test. No outgoing process response or worker reconnection. The contact result remains available through contemporary human radio reports.

After either response, show a separate confirmation with those recipients and content. Confirm commits the named action once; cancel sends nothing and keeps the case open. No source playback or sympathetic expression substitutes for agreement. In any branch with no outbound traffic, state "No external transmission" explicitly.

## CC-S05: the room afterward

AFTERMATH + PLAY. Enter through the next explicit household check-in. Show the selected trace, or a neutral pending-case lead if no outcome was committed. Do not wait for elapsed wall-clock time.

- After CC-O01: A shared contact ledger and a restricted process-input card remain at reception. The system has a provisional place in the practice, not authority over it.
- After CC-O02: The shared contact ledger names human operators and pending reports. The process proposal is retained in a closed evidence folder, not erased or secretly answered.

| Line | Speaker | Text |

| CC-L011 | Jo | I saved you a place. |
| CC-L012 | Player | At the desk? |

| CC-L014 | Ren | When you are ready, someone at Orchard wants to say hello. |

The confirmed contact test produces CC-T02 only now. Before outcome confirmation, this source does not exist and cannot be acquired. If the test is postponed, the result remains pending.

### CC-T02

Present-day human operators' ordinary radio exchange, recorded with their agreement.

> Orchard: Our endpoint heard South Relay.

> South Relay: Ours heard Orchard.

> Crew: Reported contact only. No travel result.

> Ren: Those are the words going in the shared ledger.


The exchange is present-day. Acknowledge the aftermath once. Leaving the room retains it; replay does not repeat a send or rewrite the outcome.

## Carry-forward and staging limits

Show each earlier completed trace in its actual branch form. No optional confession is required. Jo's son remains of unknown fate. An unresolved episode supplies a neutral prop and optional return lead, never an assumed outcome.

Resolve the season's question of who may decide and what a useful fix looks like. A later ordinary call supplies a season-two opening without a timer, forced acceptance, homecoming, or game-ending explanation.

No final assets, licensed music, actual electronics procedure, external services, or cinematics package is specified. A later implementation issue must select concrete interaction targets and test the claimed privacy and access, rather than assuming a quiet corner is private.
