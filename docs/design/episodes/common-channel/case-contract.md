# Common channel: facts and branches

[Brief](README.md) · [Script](script.md). IDs below are authoring references, not Unity APIs.

## Author truth and chronology

The intelligence assembled the plan from available maintenance records and contemporary reports. The inspected short links are genuine; one proposed continuation comes from stale records and is not a verified current route. Human operators identify the mismatch. The system cannot cross or repair that physical gap by declaring a topology. The season's achievable fix is a consent-based protocol for checking connected paths and exchanging observations, not restoration of the world.

The original events described in stored sources precede their arrival here. Present-day accounts follow local receipt and inspection. Original device labels, receipt order, author chronology, and character interpretation are separate. Stored sources replay identically. Contemporary replies are to contemporary people or the explicitly identified current endpoint; no participant in a stored recording is contacted through playback.

The exact reveal is: The household can build a useful present-day network practice without recovering the old geography or accepting an unknown system as coordinator. If contacted, the process acknowledges that human reports supply distinctions its records cannot settle. Its cause, identity, and metaphysical purpose remain concealed from the characters.

Remain unknown: Whether the process is one distributed intelligence, whether its restriction can be trusted elsewhere, and whether wider links will ever allow reliable travel. The secret project is not revealed in this finale.
