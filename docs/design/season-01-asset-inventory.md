## Visual decisions for this pass

The office has become a house through use. Keep salvaged beige shells and dark metal beside patched upholstery, labelled tools, paper, and shared food. Teal, dusty pink, and ochre mark repairs and human decisions. Their purpose is visual organization; a teal object does not prove a safe network connection. Avoid a single corporate product family or a glowing supernatural receiver design.

The off-air control is a physical switch in a reused box. The printer has a lid, feed button, output slot, and blank label strip. The microphone sits on a short weighted stand; the covered variant has an opaque cloth sleeve and visible tape. The patch lead has two free plugs. Its appearance gives the future episode a disconnection prop, not an automatic network action.

Paper objects carry blank inserts or generic operating words: LOCAL, ASK, and OFF AIR. The season needs explicit control over who can read each account. Do not put Nessa's private account, a caller identity, the son's fate, or a private confession into a decorative texture. A readable account needs its authored transcript equivalent and audience gate. A generic ASK card does not itself authorize recording or transmission.

The camera has a weather hood, lens, and mounting bracket. It supplies a plausible original signal device for Episode 1. It does not expand the camera's coverage into the basement or show the mother's bag search. Those are separate sources and accounts in the existing script.

## Next asset passes

1. Review the delivered Motion detected source setting and fixed camera coverage before staging the son's return. The source set has no actor or animated clips. His later fate remains unknown.
2. Design Jo and Min through the existing resident pipeline, with distinct silhouettes and clothing. Keep addiction-related signs out of generic face or body stereotypes; the script establishes the relationship through actions and accounts.
3. Build source readers and branch-aware trace displays from the script contracts. Use blank physical objects from this kit as their scene anchors, then test disclosure and refusal paths in each episode.
4. Compose a shared broadcast desk using the existing receiver, terminal, and new controls. Add original music and ordinary radio material in a separate audio task with documented permissions.
5. Dress the house with accumulated traces only after their outcome conditions exist. A later episode must handle a missing optional account and either earlier closing route.
